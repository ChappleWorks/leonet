04 / A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before we connect.

What does LeoNet do?

We source real estate opportunities and connect property owners with investors. Depending on the transaction, we may contract to purchase a property and assign our contractual interest to another buyer, or participate in a purchase and resale. The proposed role and terms should be discussed before you commit.

Are you a real estate listing service?

This website is for property acquisition opportunities, not an MLS or brokerage listing service. If you want to list on the open market, a licensed real estate agent can explain that route.

Do I need to fix up my property first?

You can begin a conversation about a property in its current condition. Whether a deal is a fit depends on the condition, price, title, and other details.

Are there live deals on this website?

No live inventory is published here yet. Share your buying criteria to prepare your investor profile; availability and terms are determined deal by deal.

Does starting a conversation commit me to anything?

No. Providing information is not an offer, an accepted agreement, or a guarantee of a transaction. Review any written terms before deciding to proceed.
